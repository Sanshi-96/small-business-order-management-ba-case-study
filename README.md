# Small Business Order Management — Business Analysis Case Study

## Overview

This project is a Business Analysis case study focused on improving order management for a small business that receives customer orders through multiple platforms.

The case study explores how orders can be missed or incorrectly recorded when information is handled across different communication channels, while customers have limited visibility into their order status.

The objective of this project is to analyze the business problem, identify stakeholders, define requirements, and document potential improvements from a Business Analyst perspective.

## Business Problem

The business currently receives customer orders through multiple platforms. Managing these orders across different channels can make it difficult for staff to maintain a consistent and accurate view of incoming orders.

This can result in:

- Missed orders
- Incorrectly recorded order details
- Difficulty managing orders from multiple channels
- Limited customer visibility into order progress
- Additional customer enquiries about order status

## Business Analysis Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Understand the current order-management problem
- Identify relevant stakeholders
- Understand the current process and its pain points
- Define business requirements
- Define functional requirements
- Define non-functional requirements
- Identify ambiguity and missing information
- Create a foundation for further BA artifacts such as process models, user stories, acceptance criteria, prioritization, and traceability
