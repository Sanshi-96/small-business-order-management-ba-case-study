# Small Business Order Management — Business Analysis Case Study

## Overview

This repository contains a Business Analysis case study focused on
improving the order-management process of a small business.

The business currently receives customer orders through multiple channels
such as WhatsApp, Facebook, and phone calls. Orders are manually managed,
which can create difficulties in maintaining accurate order information,
monitoring orders, and providing customers with visibility into their
order status.

This case study analyses the current business problem and proposes a
structured future-state process supported by business and functional
requirements.


## Business Problem

The business currently receives customer orders through multiple
platforms, making it difficult for staff to maintain a consistent and
accurate view of all incoming orders.

This can result in:

- Missed orders.
- Incorrectly recorded order information.
- Increased manual work.
- Difficulty monitoring order progress.
- Limited customer visibility into order status.
- Additional customer enquiries.


## Business Objectives

The proposed improvement aims to:

- Improve order information accuracy.
- Reduce the risk of missed orders.
- Improve customer order visibility.
- Provide a more structured order-management process.
- Improve monitoring of order progress.

## Key Business Requirements

### BR-01 — Improve Order Information Accuracy

Improve the accuracy and completeness of order information to reduce
errors during order processing.

### BR-02 — Reduce Missed Orders

Reduce the risk of orders being missed due to multiple order channels and
manual order management.

### BR-03 — Improve Customer Order Visibility

Improve customer visibility into the progress of submitted orders and
reduce unnecessary status enquiries.


## Key Functional Requirements

- The system should provide order confirmation after a valid order is
  successfully submitted.
- Customers should be able to select an available payment method during
  the order process.
- Authorized staff should be able to view supported orders through a
  centralized order-management interface.
- The system should automatically create and store an order record after
  successful submission.
- Customers should be able to view the current status of submitted
  orders.


## Process Analysis

### As-Is

The current process involves receiving orders through multiple channels
and manually managing order information before fulfilment.

The As-Is analysis identifies the current process, actors, pain points,
and process gaps.

### To-Be

The proposed future state introduces a more centralized order-management
process with improved order information handling, validation, order
status management, and customer visibility.

## Key Learning

The main learning from this case study was that Business Analysis is not
simply about writing requirements.

It involves understanding the problem, asking the right questions,
identifying stakeholder needs, analysing the current process, defining
the desired future state, and connecting business needs to measurable
requirements.

The analysis also highlighted the importance of identifying ambiguity,
validating assumptions, and maintaining traceability throughout the
process.
