# Problem Statement

## Background

The business currently receives customer orders through multiple platforms. Staff need to manage order information across these different channels, which can make the order-management process difficult to maintain consistently.

## Identified Problems

Based on the initial analysis, the following problems were identified:

### 1. Orders are received through multiple platforms

Staff need to monitor different communication channels to identify incoming orders. This creates a risk of missing orders when information is not monitored or transferred consistently.

### 2. Order details may be missed or recorded incorrectly

When order information is manually handled across different platforms, there is a possibility of missing order details or recording incorrect information.

### 3. Customers have limited order visibility

Customers may not receive sufficient information about the progress of their orders after placing them.

This can result in customers needing to contact the business to ask about their order status.

## Problem Statement

The business currently receives customer orders through multiple platforms, making it difficult for staff to maintain a consistent and accurate view of all incoming orders. This can result in missed or incorrectly recorded order details. Customers also have limited visibility into their order status after placing an order, which may lead to additional enquiries and communication with staff.

## Initial Business Impact

The identified problems may contribute to:

- Missed customer orders
- Incorrect order processing
- Increased manual work for staff
- Difficulty monitoring order progress
- Additional customer enquiries
- Reduced visibility of the overall order process

## Initial Analysis Note

The above problem statement represents the initial understanding of the business problem. Further stakeholder elicitation and process analysis are required to validate the problem, identify root causes, and understand the current As-Is process.
