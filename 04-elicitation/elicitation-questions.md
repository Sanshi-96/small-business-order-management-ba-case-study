# Elicitation Questions

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of these elicitation questions is to gather information from
relevant stakeholders about the current order-management process, existing
problems, stakeholder needs, and expected outcomes.

The questions are designed to understand the current situation before
defining or proposing a solution.

---

## 2. Elicitation Approach

The questions are grouped into the following areas:

1. Current Situation
2. Current Process
3. Pain Points and Problems
4. Root Causes
5. Stakeholder Needs
6. Order Communication
7. Exceptions and Data Issues
8. Desired Outcomes

---

## 3. Current Situation

### EQ-01
What problems are you currently experiencing with the order-management process?

**Purpose:**  
To understand the stakeholder's overall view of the current problem.

### EQ-02
Which platforms are currently used to receive customer orders?

**Purpose:**  
To identify the different channels through which orders enter the business.

---

## 4. Current Process

### EQ-03
Can you walk me through the current order process from receiving an order
to completing the delivery?

**Purpose:**  
To understand the end-to-end current process and identify the activities,
roles, and handoffs involved.

### EQ-04
How is an order currently received, recorded, processed, and communicated
between the people involved?

**Purpose:**  
To understand how order information moves through the business.

---

## 5. Pain Points and Problems

### EQ-05
At which stage are orders most frequently missed or incorrectly recorded?

**Purpose:**  
To identify where problems occur within the current process.

### EQ-06
What problems do customers and staff experience with the current
order-management process?

**Purpose:**  
To identify pain points from both internal and external stakeholder
perspectives.

---

## 6. Root Causes

### EQ-07
What are the main reasons staff experience difficulties when managing
orders?

**Purpose:**  
To explore the possible causes behind the identified problems rather than
only documenting the symptoms.

### EQ-08
Are there any specific situations that commonly result in missing or
incorrect order information?

**Purpose:**  
To identify recurring situations, conditions, or exceptions that contribute
to order-management problems.

---

## 7. Order Communication

### EQ-09
How is an order currently confirmed and communicated to the customer?

**Purpose:**  
To understand the existing customer confirmation process.

### EQ-10
How do customers currently find out the status or progress of their orders?

**Purpose:**  
To understand the current level of customer visibility after an order is
submitted.

---

## 8. Stakeholder Needs

### EQ-11
Who are the different users or staff members involved in the order process?

**Purpose:**  
To identify stakeholders who interact with or are affected by the process.

### EQ-12
What information does each person need in order to perform their part of
the order process effectively?

**Purpose:**  
To understand information needs across different stakeholder groups.

---

## 9. Exceptions and Data Issues

### EQ-13
What happens when order information is missing, incomplete, or incorrect?

**Purpose:**  
To understand how exceptions are currently handled.

### EQ-14
What happens when a customer changes or cancels an order after it has been
submitted?

**Purpose:**  
To identify additional process scenarios and exception handling needs.

---

## 10. Desired Outcomes

### EQ-15
What would a successful order-management process look like after the
identified problems are improved?

**Purpose:**  
To understand stakeholder expectations and define potential business
outcomes.

### EQ-16
What should definitely not happen in the improved order-management process?

**Purpose:**  
To identify unacceptable outcomes, risks, and constraints.

---

## 11. Expected Elicitation Outcomes

The answers to these questions will be used to identify:

- Current-state process
- Pain points
- Root causes
- Stakeholders
- Business needs
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Process improvement opportunities
- Exceptions and business rules
- Desired business outcomes

Further clarification may be required where stakeholder responses are
ambiguous, incomplete, conflicting, or inconsistent.
