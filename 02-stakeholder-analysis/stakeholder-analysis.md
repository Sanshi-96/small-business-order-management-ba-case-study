# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify individuals or groups who are affected by the current order-management problem, participate in the order process, or have an interest in the proposed change.

## Identified Stakeholders

| ID | Stakeholder | Relationship to the Problem | Main Interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST-01 | Customers | Place orders and require information about order progress | Easy ordering and order-status visibility |
| ST-02 | Sales / Order Staff | Receive and manage customer orders | Accurate and centralized order information |
| ST-03 | Packing Staff | Prepare customer orders | Correct order details and clear order information |
| ST-04 | Delivery Staff | Deliver completed orders | Accurate delivery information and order status |
| ST-05 | Business Owner / Manager | Responsible for business operations and customer experience | Reduced missed orders and improved operational visibility |
| ST-06 | Payment / Finance Staff | Handle or verify customer payments | Accurate payment and order information |
| ST-07 | System Development Team | Responsible for implementing the solution | Clear and feasible requirements |

## Stakeholder Observations

The stakeholders have different perspectives and needs.

For example:

- Customers are primarily concerned with ordering and receiving status information.
- Order staff are concerned with receiving and managing orders accurately.
- Packing and delivery staff require accurate order information to complete their tasks.
- Business owners are concerned with operational efficiency and customer experience.
- The development team requires clear and testable requirements.

## Stakeholder Validation

The stakeholder list is based on the initial analysis of the scenario. In a real project, the Business Analyst would validate the stakeholder list with the business and identify additional stakeholders who may influence or be affected by the change.
