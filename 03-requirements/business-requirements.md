# Business Requirements

## Purpose

Business requirements describe the high-level needs and outcomes the business expects to achieve through the proposed change.

They focus on **why the change is needed**, rather than describing specific system features.

## Business Requirements

### BR-01 — Improve Order Information Accuracy

The business needs to improve the accuracy and completeness of customer order information throughout the order-management process.

**Business Goal:**

Reduce errors caused by manually transferring or recording order information.

---

### BR-02 — Reduce Missed Orders

The business needs to reduce the occurrence of customer orders being missed due to orders being received through multiple communication platforms.

**Business Goal:**

Improve visibility and control over incoming customer orders.

---

### BR-03 — Improve Customer Order Visibility

The business needs to improve customer visibility into the progress of their submitted orders.

**Business Goal:**

Reduce unnecessary customer enquiries and improve the transparency of the order process.

## Business Requirement Summary

| ID | Business Requirement | Expected Business Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Improve order information accuracy | Fewer order information errors |
| BR-02 | Reduce missed orders | Better control of incoming orders |
| BR-03 | Improve customer order visibility | Better customer awareness of order progress |

## Validation Note

These business requirements are based on the initial problem analysis. In a real business engagement, they would need to be validated with the relevant stakeholders before being formally approved.
